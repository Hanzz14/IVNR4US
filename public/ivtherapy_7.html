Information

Chemotherapy and Liver Disease Support

Methylene blue mitigates the toxic effects of certain chemotherapy drugs and improves tissue oxygenation. It enhances blood flow and prevents hypotension in severe liver diseases like cirrhosis and during major surgeries.

Antimicrobial and Anti-inflammatory Properties

Methylene blue combats fungal, viral, and bacterial infections, aids in overcoming drug resistance, and reduces inflammation. It supports conditions like colitis, osteoarthritis, and tick-borne illnesses, and helps in stroke and traumatic brain injury recovery.

Brain and Cellular Health

This therapy enhances brain function, memory, and mood by improving mitochondrial function and cellular respiration. It also acts as an antioxidant, protecting cells from oxidative stress and aiding in anti-aging and chronic disease prevention.
