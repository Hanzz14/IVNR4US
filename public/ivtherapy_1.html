Need for Supplementation

Adults need 65–75 mg of vitamin C daily, but higher amounts may be required for older adults or those with chronic conditions. The Western diet often lacks sufficient vitamin C.

Benefits of IV Vitamin C

IV vitamin C provides 100% bioavailability, offering quick action and higher plasma levels compared to oral supplements. It supports immune function, reduces oxidative stress, and helps manage chronic ailments.
