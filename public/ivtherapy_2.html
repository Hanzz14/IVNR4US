Information

Superior Absorption and Efficacy

IV infusion achieves supraphysiological vitamin C plasma levels, far exceeding what is possible with oral intake. This enhanced absorption allows for unique therapeutic effects and higher efficacy in managing health conditions.

Cancer Support and Antioxidant Properties

High-dose IV vitamin C may reduce chemotherapy side effects, improve quality of life, and slow tumor growth. It also offers stronger antioxidant protection against oxidative stress and cellular damage, benefiting those with chronic ailments.

Enhanced Immune and Neuroprotective Effects

High doses boost white blood cell function, enhancing immune response and potentially managing severe infections. They also offer neuroprotective benefits, reducing the risk of neurodegenerative diseases like Alzheimer’s and Parkinson’s, and supporting cardiovascular health.
